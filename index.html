INTERACTIVE MYSTERY

Before Midnight

A rain-soaked train and a loop in time.

Every choice has a cost

When detective Lin Yan hears a recording from his missing sister, he follows an impossible ticket to a hidden platform. The last train waits beneath the city, frozen at 11:59.

Question a traveler who knows your future, examine clues from abandoned timelines, and decide which reality is allowed to survive after midnight.

Features

  • A cinematic, character-led interactive mystery
  • Branching decisions and multiple endings
  • Atmospheric original artwork and clue-based storytelling
  • Music, sound effects, vibration, and accessible tap-to-advance play
  • Playable without an account
