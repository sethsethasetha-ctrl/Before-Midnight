A narrative puzzle game

SagaSprint

A café paused just before goodbye.

One afternoon, again and again

Step into a neighborhood café on its final day of business. At 6:00 PM, time quietly folds back to 3:00 PM. The room resets, its visitors forget, and only you remember what happened.

Explore the café, notice what has changed, and gather the small clues hidden in ordinary things: an unfinished sketch, a saved message, a recipe card, a letter never signed.

Help everyone move forward

Three people are holding on to a tomorrow they are afraid to face. Through careful observation and thoughtful conversation, help them find the words that let them take their next step—and discover why you cannot leave this day behind.

  • Explore a hand-crafted café across a repeating afternoon.
  • Collect clues that open new conversations and choices.
  • Follow a gentle, character-led story with multiple endings.
  • Enjoy a quiet puzzle experience with no irreversible failures.
