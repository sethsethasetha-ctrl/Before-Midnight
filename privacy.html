Privacy Policy

Before Midnight · Effective date: September 4, 2026

Before Midnight is developed by Junhao Hong. This policy explains how the app handles information when you play.

Information stored on your device

The app stores gameplay progress and your sound, music, and vibration preferences locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the game experience and is not used to identify you.

Advertising and tracking permission

The app may display rewarded advertisements between story chapters. iOS asks for permission before allowing tracking for personalized advertising. You may allow or decline this request. Declining does not prevent you from playing the game. Advertising partners may process device information in accordance with their own privacy policies when advertisements are shown.

Children

Before Midnight is not directed to children under the age at which consent is required in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not require an account, and the game does not ask you to provide your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location to play.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the developer through the app's App Store support channel.
